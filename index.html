Architectural-Compliant 3D Building Generation with Compositional Geometry from Single-Image

Anonymous authors · paper under review
📄 Paper (soon) 💻 Code (soon) 📦 Data (soon) ✎ Participate in our Human Study

Abstract

We present an agentic pipeline that generates a complete multi-storey residential building — envelope, roof, and unseen interior layouts — from a single exterior photograph, with every architectural element emitted as a cleanly separable, semantically labeled mesh component. Existing approaches fall short of this goal: procedural systems demand manual encoding of every design rule and produce over-simplified interiors; learned generative models offer no validity guarantees; and the multi-floor setting, where staircases and structural walls couple floors vertically, remains largely unexplored.

Our key insight is twofold. First, architectural knowledge is native to 2D: we adopt the vector floor plan as the intermediate representation in which design rules can be stated, checked, and repaired, and from which 3D geometry follows procedurally, making the output decomposable by construction. Second, we organize heterogeneous architectural constraints — from verifiable geometric predicates to fuzzy design heuristics — into a four-level hierarchy of atomic natural-language rules that serve as a single source of truth for both generation and verification. A Generate–Judge–Repair–Fix loop enforces these rules at every pipeline stage, combining programmatic validators, deterministic repairs, and vision-language judging.

Method

Highlights

  • Single photo in, full building out — multi-storey envelope, roof, and interiors, all floors stair-consistent.
  • Compositional geometry — walls, openings, stairs, roof, and room volumes as separate, labeled mesh components.
  • Rules as a single source of truth — the same natural-language rule drives generation, verification, and repair.
  • Generate–Judge–Repair–Fix — programmatic validators and VLM judges gate every stage of the pipeline.
  • Comprehensive evaluation — procedural (Infinigen-Indoors), learned 2D (GSDiff), and direct LLM baselines, plus an architect / non-architect human study.
